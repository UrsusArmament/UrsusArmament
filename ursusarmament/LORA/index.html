IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR PREPARING AND USING YOUR T114 BOARDS

Before You Apply Power

  • Attach the antenna first. Firmly secure the LoRa antenna to the board before connecting USB, battery or solar power. Powering or transmitting without it can damage the radio chip.
  • Remove all power before changing hardware. Disconnect USB, battery and solar before attaching or removing the antenna or GNSS module.
  • Use the right antenna. Use an antenna made for the 902–928 MHz band.
  • Keep conductive items away. Don’t power the board while it rests on metal or near loose screws, wire or tools.

Battery and Solar Power

  • Check polarity every time. A battery plug that fits may still be wired backward. Match positive and negative to the board markings before connecting.
  • Single cell only. The battery input is for 3–4.2 V. Never connect cells in series. Use a protected lithium battery.
  • Solar input is 5–6.5 V. A panel of about 5 V is recommended. Check polarity and don’t connect solar to the battery connector.
  • Don’t charge a frozen battery. Charge only within the battery maker’s temperature limits, typically above 0 °C (32 °F). The board’s operating range doesn’t set your battery’s limits.
  • Stop using damaged batteries. Don’t use a battery that is swollen, punctured, crushed or overheating.

Handling

  • Connectors are small. Align them before pressing, don’t force them, and pull on the connector body rather than the wires. Take extra care with the antenna connector and lead.
  • Protect against static and shorts. Hold the board by its edges and avoid touching components.
  • A bare board is not weatherproof. Protect it from water, condensation, dirt and impact. Let it dry fully before applying power if condensation forms, such as after coming in from the cold.

Setup and Flashing

  • Boards may arrive preloaded. You don’t need to flash a board that already has the firmware you want.
  • Use a USB data cable. Charge-only cables won’t work. Use Chrome or Edge for web flashing.
  • Select the exact board. Choose the Heltec T114 option and the correct firmware type. Don’t unplug the board while it is flashing.
  • Back up first. Erasing or switching firmware can remove settings, keys and messages.
  • If the board isn’t detected, try double-pressing the RST button to enter bootloader (DFU) mode.

Radio Settings

  • Meshtastic: Set the LoRa region before use. In the United States, select US.
  • MeshCore: In the United States, start with the USA/Canada (Recommended) preset. If your local mesh uses different settings, match them within legal limits.
  • The two don’t communicate. Meshtastic and MeshCore boards can’t message each other. Boards on the same firmware also need matching radio settings.
  • Follow local rules. Frequency and transmit power limits vary by location.

GNSS (GPS) Module

  • Connect with power removed. Use the 8-pin GNSS connector and check its alignment before seating it.
  • Give it a view of the sky. A first position fix can take several minutes and may fail indoors.
  • Check the app. Make sure GPS is turned on after installing or moving the module.

Privacy and Field Use

  • Public channels aren’t private. Default public channels use publicly known keys. Use a private channel with its own key for your group.
  • Check location sharing. Review position settings before going on air so you don’t share your location unintentionally.
  • Test before you rely on it. Range depends on terrain, obstructions, antenna placement and nearby nodes.
  • Keep a backup. Don’t depend on these boards as your only means of emergency communication.
