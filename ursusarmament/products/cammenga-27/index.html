CAMMENGA MODEL 27

Phosphorescent Lensatic Compass

Cammenga Model 27 lensatic compass featuring phosphorescent illumination for low-light navigation. Aluminum construction, suitable for navigation, field use, collecting, or military surplus applications.

Illumination Note: The Model 27 uses phosphorescent (glow-in-the-dark) illumination rather than tritium. It must be "charged" by light exposure to glow and does not contain a radioactive source, so there is no long-term decay curve to account for.
