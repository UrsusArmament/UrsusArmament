About URSUS WORKS™ ENGINEERING

Ursus Works™ is a veteran-owned and operated company. We got into the production industry to give ourselves the capability to manufacture parts and boards domestically for our other divisions of URSUS WORKS™, with the intent to assist our country in bringing American manufacturing back to the states.

Our work is developed with a focus on real-world application, where reliability, simplicity, and performance matter more than features on paper.

Mission

Purpose Driven

Ursus Works™ Engineering exists to produce the parts and circuit boards our other divisions depend on, right here in the United States. Our focus is on reliable, high-quality production grounded in real-world use rather than theoretical conditions.

We build what we would trust ourselves, and we intend to help bring American manufacturing back to the states.

Environment

Alaska Origins

Founded in Alaska, Ursus Works™ reflects the demands of harsh and unpredictable environments. What we build is designed with temperature extremes, exposure, and sustained use in mind, conditions that quickly expose weaknesses in both materials and design.

Approach

No Excess

Where possible, what we build is made to be serviceable and repairable, reducing dependency and extending lifespan.

Components are sourced from vetted suppliers and subjected to strict quality control standards. Assembly is performed domestically, with partial domestic production today and a clear objective of moving toward fully domestic manufacturing over time. Ursus Works™ Engineering is how we get there.

We avoid unnecessary complexity and marketing-driven features. Every design decision serves a purpose, prioritizing reliability, serviceability, and long-term performance.

Privacy

Privacy & Security First

Why we keep so little: The safest data is data we never collect. In an industry where customer privacy matters, we hold only what's needed to fulfill your order and honor your warranty. Information we don't have can't be breached, leaked, or misused.

Payments: All payments are processed by Stripe. Ursus Works™ never sees, stores, or logs your card information.

Your data: Order information is stored off site on secure cloud infrastructure, separate from the website itself, and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorized Ursus personnel.

Reviews: Reviews are stored off site and show only a first name and state.

Warranty: To honor your warranty, we keep only the bare minimum: your date of purchase, the product and serial number (if applicable), and the email used for the purchase. Once a warranty expires, we delete the personal information tied to that purchase whenever able. We retain only the serialized record of the unit itself (serial number and product specifications), which supports our unit tracking.

Direction

Ongoing Development

In the near term, development focuses on building up in-house production of the parts and circuit boards our other divisions depend on. Down the road, we plan to offer quality domestic manufacturing to American companies that need, or simply prefer, to keep their business at home.
